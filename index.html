About Me

Hello! My name is Daniel-Adrian Petrescu, and I am a third-year Electronics Engineering student at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

I am passionate about Embedded Systems, FPGA Design, IoT, software development and modern technologies. I enjoy designing practical projects using Arduino, ESP32, FPGA and SQL while continuously expanding my technical knowledge.

Beyond engineering, I enjoy reading, listening to music and challenging myself to learn something new every day. I strongly believe that continuous learning and self-improvement are the keys to becoming both a better engineer and a better person.

ESP32-CAM – Mobile Video Monitoring System

Multi-camera monitoring system built with four ESP32-CAM modules and PIR motion sensors. The system provides real-time video streaming, automatic motion detection and photo capture, with instant Telegram notifications for detected events. A web interface allows all four camera feeds to be monitored simultaneously over Wi-Fi.
