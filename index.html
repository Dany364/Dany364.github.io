About Me

Hello! My name is Daniel Petrescu, and I am a second-year Electronics Engineering student at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

I am passionate about Embedded Systems, FPGA Design, IoT, software development and modern technologies. I enjoy designing practical projects using Arduino, ESP32, FPGA, Python and SQL while continuously expanding my technical knowledge.

Beyond engineering, I enjoy reading, listening to music and challenging myself to learn something new every day. I strongly believe that continuous learning and self-improvement are the keys to becoming both a better engineer and a better person.
